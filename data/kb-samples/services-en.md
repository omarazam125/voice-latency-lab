# Service Guide — Al Mada Bank

## Available services

- **Current accounts**: no minimum balance, free ATM card included.
- **Savings accounts**: up to 3.5 percent annual return, calculated monthly.
- **Local and international transfers**: instant within the kingdom, international within one business day.
- **Credit cards**: Visa and Mastercard, credit limits starting at 5,000 riyals.
- **Personal finance**: up to 500,000 riyals, repayment terms up to 60 months.
- **Home finance**: up to 90 percent of the property value.

## Opening hours

Branches are open Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM.
The call centre is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week, on 800-123-4567.
Digital services in the app are available 24 hours a day.

## Opening a new account

You will need:

1. A valid national ID or residency permit.
2. A completed account opening form.
3. Proof of national address.

The account is activated within 24 hours of the documents being completed.
There is no fee to open an account.

## Fees

- Replacement ATM card: 30 riyals.
- International transfer: 50 riyals per transaction.
- Printed account statement: 25 riyals.
- Local transfers through the app: free.
- Minimum balance on a current account: none.

## Lost or stolen cards

If a card is lost or stolen, call the contact centre immediately on 800-123-4567,
or freeze the card directly in the app. A replacement card is issued within
3 business days.

## Personal finance details

- Amount: from 10,000 riyals to 500,000 riyals.
- Term: from 12 months to 60 months.
- Annual profit rate: starting from 4.25 percent.
- Conditions: the customer must be employed with a monthly income of at least
  4,000 riyals and must have completed at least 3 months in their current job.
- Approval time: a decision is given within 48 hours of application.

## Complaints

Complaints can be raised in the app or by calling the contact centre.
Complaints receive a response within a maximum of 5 business days.
A complaint reference number is sent by SMS as soon as it is logged.
